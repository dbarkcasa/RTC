Bedford, TX, USA

Location

Bedford is located in the heart of the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex in Texas.

About

Weather

Bedford enjoys hot summers and mild winters, with plenty of sunny days throughout the year.

Population

Over 49,000 people call Bedford home.

Sports

Soccer, baseball, and football are popular sports in Bedford, with many local leagues for all ages.

Things To Do

  • Visit Bedford Boys Ranch Park
  • Enjoy hiking and biking on local trails
  • Attend the annual Bedford Blues & BBQ Festival
